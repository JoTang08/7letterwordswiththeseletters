Tips for Finding Five-Letter Words

  • In Wordle, start with letters that cover the most common positions: S, E, A, R, O, T, I, N appear most frequently in five-letter answers.
  • Look for common endings: -TION, -NESS, -IGHT, -OUND, -ATCH — many five-letter words end in these patterns.
  • Vowel-heavy hands (3+ vowels) often yield words like AUDIO, ADIEU, LOUIE, OAKEN — enter them and let the solver surface options.

Common Five-Letter Patterns

Certain five-letter combinations appear far more often than others because they're built from the most common English letters. STARE, CRANE, and TRACE are popular Wordle opening guesses precisely because they cover A, E, R, T, C, N, S — six of the highest-frequency letters. Knowing these openers and their anagrams gives you a measurable edge in daily play.

In Scrabble, five-letter words ending in -S are especially valuable — they can extend existing words on the board and often hit two premium squares in one play. Words with Q or Z at five letters are rarer but score dramatically more: QUAFF, JAZZY, FIZZY can swing an entire game when placed on a triple-word square.
