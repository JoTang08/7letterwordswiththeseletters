Why Nine-Letter Words Matter

In Scrabble, nine-letter plays require hooking onto two existing board tiles while using all seven rack tiles — a rare but high-reward situation. In crossword puzzles, nine-letter entries are extremely common, appearing in nearly every American broadsheet grid. Knowing nine-letter words by pattern makes you faster at both games.

Tips for Finding Nine-Letter Words

  • Prefixes RE-, UN-, OUT-, OVER-, UNDER- combined with five- or six-letter roots often produce nine-letter words.
  • Suffixes -TION, -NESS, -MENT, -ABLE, -IBLE, -ATING, -ATING extend shorter stems to nine letters.
  • In crosswords, nine-letter entries frequently use double-letter sequences — look for -TION, -NESS, and -LESS endings.
  • Use ? wildcards freely — nine-letter combinations are dense and a single blank often unlocks many new words.
