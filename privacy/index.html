Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This privacy policy explains how 7 Letter Words With These Letters (7letterwordswiththeseletters.com) handles your information when you use our word finder tool.

No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The word finder runs entirely in your browser — the letters you type are never sent to any server. When you close or navigate away from the page, nothing about your session is retained.

No Accounts Required

Using this tool requires no registration, login, or account of any kind. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information.

Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising. If cookies are set in the future (for example, to remember a user preference), this policy will be updated to reflect that.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as aggregate page view counts) to understand how the tool is used. If analytics are in use, they do not track individual users or collect personally identifiable information.

Third-Party Services

This site does not embed third-party advertising, social tracking pixels, or external scripts that collect user data. Any external resources (fonts, libraries) loaded by this page are standard, widely-used assets with their own privacy terms.

Children's Privacy

This tool is a general-purpose word game utility suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and since we collect no data from any user, no special handling is required.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
